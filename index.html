Hi! I am a CS Ph.D. candidate at UC Berkeley in Berkeley AI Research (BAIR), working with Prof. David Wagner. My research is supported by funding from the NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship, Meta FAIR and Google DeepMind through BAIR Commons, and UC Berkeley EECS.

I study AI security in real-world applications. Currently, I am defending against prompt-injection attacks, the top threat to AI agents.
