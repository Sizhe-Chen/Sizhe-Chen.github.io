Hi! I am a CS Ph.D. candidate at UC Berkeley in Berkeley AI Research (BAIR), working with Prof. David Wagner. I study real-world AI security problems with general and principled solutions. I led the development of StruQ, SecAlign, Meta-SecAlign, and SecOPD, the most robust open LLMs against adaptive prompt injections. My research has been supported by selective industry fundings, including NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship, Meta FAIR and Google DeepMind through BAIR Commons. Previously, I obtained an M.Eng. and a B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
